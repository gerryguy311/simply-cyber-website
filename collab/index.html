Questions we get asked

What does this cost?add

Rates depend on the format, the length of the commitment, and how much production is involved, so we do not publish a price list. Send a note describing the campaign goal and we will come back with a scoped number, usually within a day or two.

Do we get to write the ad copy?add

You supply the key messages, the positioning, and the call to action, and we agree all of it in advance. The delivery stays in Gerald's voice and the opinions stay his. Scripted vendor copy read word for word does not work on this audience and we will not do it.

Can we reuse the content in our own marketing?add

Yes. Produced video comes with a non transferable license so your team can repurpose it. Conference work comes with 12 month usage rights across your marketing channels. Extended and multi year licensing is available.

Will you run our competitor at the same time?add

Category exclusivity is available on some formats. Say the word early and we will hold your category for the term of the campaign.

How far ahead do we need to book?add

Threat Brief sponsorship sells in monthly blocks and the calendar fills ahead, so ask for available months as early as you can. Conference slots are capped per event and typically go first. Produced video needs lead time for the demo, the trial, and the edit.

Do you do speaking engagements too?add

That is a separate thing. If you are looking to book Gerald to speak at your event, the speaker kit lives at gerald.media.

Tell us what you are trying to accomplish.

Send the campaign goal, the timeframe, and roughly what you have to work with. We will tell you honestly whether Simply Cyber can move the needle on it, and what that would look like.
